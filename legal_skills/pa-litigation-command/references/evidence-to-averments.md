# Evidence to Numbered Averments

Convert source evidence into filing-ready factual paragraphs without adding argument or invented material.

## Email/thread method

For each discrete email/exchange, use exactly one establishing paragraph followed by separate statement paragraphs.

The establishing paragraph states, when available: date, time, sender name/email, recipient name/email, subject, attachment, and exhibit citation. It contains no quoted speech.

Each distinct quoted statement then receives its own numbered paragraph: speaker + attribution verb + verbatim quote + exhibit citation. Do not repeat sender/recipient addresses or subject in each quote paragraph.

A new date or subject requires a new establishing paragraph. If a quoted statement has its own timestamp not covered by the establishing paragraph, include that time in its paragraph.

## Other evidence types

- **Court order:** establish court, date, docket/order, then separate each relevant directive/finding into its own paragraph. Distinguish a court finding from Plaintiff's allegation.
- **Call/text/chat:** establish participants/date/source, then one material statement per paragraph. Preserve timestamps if present.
- **Video/photo:** state what is objectively visible/audible and source metadata. Do not infer motive or identity without evidentiary support.
- **Receipt/record:** establish issuer/date/record, then one material transaction or field per paragraph when needed.
- **Third-party statement:** identify the source and preserve hearsay limitations; do not convert the speaker's assertion into an adjudicated fact.

## Quote integrity

Quotes are verbatim. Preserve errors in the source. Never invent a missing quote as `"___."` or other placeholder. If a quote is unavailable, omit it from filing-ready text and mark the source gap outside the filing.

If an exhibit number is unknown in a working draft, `(Exhibit ___)` may be used temporarily, but every unresolved placeholder must be resolved before the document is labeled filing-ready.

## Paragraph discipline

- third person/past tense when drafting a complaint chronology;
- one material unit per paragraph;
- do not combine multiple speakers/events merely because they occurred in one email;
- repeat the exhibit citation on every paragraph derived from that exhibit;
- keep legal argument/case law out of pure fact paragraphs;
- flag numbering gaps instead of silently continuing;
- for multi-sentence quotes, split by sentence or natural idea break when doing so preserves exact wording and context.

## Final integrity check

Before accepting the averments, confirm each paragraph against the source for date, time, sender/speaker, quotation, exhibit number, and paragraph numbering.
