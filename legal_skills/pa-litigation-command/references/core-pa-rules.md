# Core Pennsylvania Pleading and Filing Rules

Use this as a drafting checklist, then verify the current official text before filing.

## Rule 1019 — Contents of pleadings

Draft material facts in concise and summary form. A sufficiency review should identify who, what, when, where, and how, and should identify writings relied upon when the claim or defense is based on a writing.

Do not replace facts with labels or conclusions. If the pleading says `negligent`, `reckless`, `fraudulent`, `unauthorized`, or similar, make sure numbered paragraphs state the underlying conduct.

## Rule 1020 — Separate causes of action and alternative pleading

Keep distinct causes of action in separate counts with their own requested relief. Alternative theories may be pleaded when appropriate, but the document should make the alternatives intelligible and avoid internally inconsistent factual assertions unless the alternative is expressly framed.

## Rule 1022 — Numbering

Use consecutively numbered paragraphs. As far as practicable, one material allegation belongs in each paragraph. Avoid silently skipping or duplicating paragraph numbers.

## Rule 1024 — Verification

Determine whether the filing must be verified, who may sign, and whether the verification language matches the rule and the actual basis of knowledge. Do not add a verification mechanically to every filing.

## Rule 204.1 and local presentation rules

Final presentation should conform to statewide form requirements and applicable local Schuylkill County rules. Confirm caption, margins, paper size, signature/contact block, proposed order, notice-to-defend requirements, and any praecipe or certification required for the specific motion/filing type.

## Discovery

Discovery deadlines and consequences must be derived from the governing discovery rule, service event, order, or stipulation actually applicable to the request. Do not import a pleading extension into discovery by assumption.

For requests for admission, verify Rule 4014 from the current official Chapter 4000 source before characterizing a request as admitted or conclusively established.
