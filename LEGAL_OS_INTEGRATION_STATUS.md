# Legal OS status — 2026-09-28

The static `web/` entrypoint is a local encrypted matter workspace. It preserves original uploaded files, SHA-256 hashes, extracted text and edits in passphrase-encrypted IndexedDB on one browser/device. The SAC DOCX was inspected locally: Mammoth extracted about 192,000 text characters and 670 Word list items. Direct DOCX inspection confirmed that all 670 are level-zero items of the same Word numbering definition within the operative complaint (document paragraphs 80–799, with unnumbered headings interspersed). The browser export still requires an explicit source-item range as a review safeguard. Direct inspection of the supplied DOCX located the operative complaint beginning at its caption and identified Counts I through VII, including Count II negligence per se and Count VII racial discrimination in contract enforcement. Do not reduce this pleading to an older five-count description. Text-based PDF exhibits are extracted locally; image-only scans require OCR. No SAC or private exhibit is committed to this public repository.

This is a functional local review tool, not a completed legal accuracy verification or a filing-ready complaint. Numbering reconstruction, exhibit presence, missing/gap checks, and exact quoted-text matching cannot establish the context or accuracy of facts, quotations, exhibits, citations, or authority currency. An encrypted backup can be downloaded and restored in a browser without an existing vault; the passphrase is still required. The static workspace is served at `/app/` on the production Vercel deployment; no private cross-device synchronization or owner login has been implemented. Browser storage can be lost. The Python API is separate: its hosted legal routes are disabled on Vercel unless a long owner token is configured and supplied as a bearer token. Its in-memory source index is not durable matter storage. The older Express prototype is not offered as an authenticated matter service.
